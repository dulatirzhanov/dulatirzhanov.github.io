About the work

I work with leadership teams in education at the point where a decision is already being discussed and its consequences cannot be reversed without loss.

I do not provide quick advice, recommendations, or evaluations. This work starts when there is internal doubt about the chosen course and the price of a wrong decision is already clear.

My background includes leading CIS accreditation processes, working inside complex school systems, and supporting leadership teams through moments of institutional change.

If you are looking for ideas, tools, or confirmation of an already-made decision, this is not the right conversation.
