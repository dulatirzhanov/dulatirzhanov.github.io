About

I have worked in rural and urban schools, in the Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools system and its central office, and I take part in international school accreditation processes.

Quality in a school comes from decisions, and the best decisions I have seen were made by leaders who knew how other systems had solved the same problem. I write about that international practice and build tools that make it usable for school leaders.

I work in Kazakh, Russian and English, so practice from abroad reaches readers already adapted to their context.
